**Serverless Usage Policy:** Account-Level OBjects used to attribute costs by applying custom tags to serverless notebooks , jobs and pipelines and apps.
These tgs appear in billing records (system.billing.usage) to help track spending by team or project. They do not conifgure infrastructure settings.

**Compute Policies:** Used to restrict permissions and enforce configuration limits (like instance types, autoscaling, and cluster sizes) for **classic compute resources**. They are not applicable to serverless compute, which is managed entirely by Databricks and does not support user-defined compute poicies or instance type selections.
